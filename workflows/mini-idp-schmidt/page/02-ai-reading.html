When does AI start?

OCR must find a known layout, all header fields, a printed item count and a confidence of 80 or more
If one of these fails and the file is an image, it goes to Haiku
Everything else skips AI and costs nothing

Two models, two jobs

Haiku (through an OpenAI-compatible gateway) reads the fields and rates its own certainty per field
gemma4 (local, through Ollama) gets the image and the values and answers yes or no per field
A disagreement or no answer is never an approval, it sends the note to a human
