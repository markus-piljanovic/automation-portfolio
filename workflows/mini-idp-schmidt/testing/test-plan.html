Mini-IDP Schmidt: test plan

As of 2026-10-29. Every case sent a real file through the real workflow with curl. Slack answers were given by hand. Runs took place on 28 October 2026 with fictional delivery notes. Durations of human cases include the time until the Slack answer. The notes in the workflow and the Slack texts are German, the language of the course project.

How to read this page
  • Every case sends one situation into the real workflow, says what should happen, and shows what did happen.
  • The case number is a letter for the topic and a number within it: A1 is the first case of group A. The groups are listed below.
  • Red cases are error or edge cases, dashed ones needed a real Slack click. Open means the case was not run yet.
